THE SHORT VERSION

Clockus works without an account, and then everything stays in your browser. If you sign in with Google, your tasks, settings and stats are saved online so they follow you to your other devices. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

02What Clockus stores

  • Without an account: your tasks, settings, theme, focus sessions and streaks are saved in your browser, on your device. They never leave it.
  • With Google sign-in: your name, email address and profile picture, which Google shares so Clockus can sign you in.
  • Also with sign-in: your tasks, settings and focus stats, saved online so they sync between your devices.
  • If you send feedback: the message and the optional email you write.

03How it is used

To sign you in, to show your own data back to you, and to keep your devices in sync. Nothing else. Your data is not used for advertising and is not sold or shared for marketing.

04Who else is involved

  • Google handles the sign-in. Clockus only receives the basic profile details listed above.
  • Supabase is the secure database where synced data is stored.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. Like any web host, it can technically see standard connection details such as an IP address.

05Cookies and storage

Clockus uses your browser’s own storage to remember your settings and to keep you signed in. There are no advertising cookies.

06Your choices

  • Use Clockus without an account, any time.
  • Sign out whenever you want, from Settings, Account and data.
  • Download your tasks and focus history with Export, in the same place.
  • To delete your synced data and account, write to [your contact email] and we delete them.
  • Clear your browser data to remove everything saved on your device.

07Changes to this policy

If this page changes in a meaningful way, the date at the top changes too.

08Contact
