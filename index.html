FEATURES

Everything you need to focus, nothing you don't.

01

Pomodoro timer

Pick 15, 25, 45 or 60 minutes, or set your own. Breaks start automatically, and you can skip them.

02

Clock

A calm full-screen clock with 12h or 24h time, optional seconds and two fonts.

03

To do

Add, edit and drag tasks into order. Finished tasks sink to the bottom and stay crossed out for the day.

04

Stats

See today, this week and every day, with a GitHub-style Focus map of the whole year.

05

Streaks

Focus 1h a day to keep your flame alive. Track your current streak and your best.

06

Sync

Sign in with Google to keep your tasks, settings and stats on every device.

  • Space · R · N shortcuts
  • Dark and light theme
  • Your own accent color
  • Mono or Inter clock font
